## Why this exists

DBRB is offered under two licences: the open-source AGPL-3.0, and a commercial licence
for organisations that need one. The commercial licence helps pay for the project. To
offer your contribution under both, the project needs your permission. That is what this
agreement gives.

**You keep ownership of your work.** You can use it anywhere else, under any terms.
